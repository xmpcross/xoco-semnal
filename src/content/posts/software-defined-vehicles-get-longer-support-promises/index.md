Automakers are extending update windows as buyers compare cars to phones, servers, and subscriptions.

## Why It Matters

Vehicle software support is becoming part of residual value.

A car with uncertain updates is harder to price, insure, and keep secure over a long ownership cycle.

## What To Watch

Expect support timelines to appear more often in EV comparison tools.
