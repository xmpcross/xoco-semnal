Finance, support, and operations teams are adopting constrained agents with narrow permissions and clear audit trails.

## Why It Matters

The agent market is maturing around bounded tasks instead of open-ended assistants.

A boring workflow with approvals, logs, and retries is easier to buy than a magical general-purpose bot.

## What To Watch

Procurement teams will ask harder questions about observability than model choice.
