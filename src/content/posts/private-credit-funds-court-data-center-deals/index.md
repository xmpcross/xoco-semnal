Debt investors are building new underwriting models for campuses where power access matters as much as tenant demand.

## Why It Matters

Data center construction has moved from niche real estate to a capital markets story.

Lenders now have to evaluate grid interconnection, long-term power contracts, cooling constraints, and whether AI tenants will keep expanding at the promised pace.

## What To Watch

Expect more deals to include staged financing tied to power delivery milestones.

The spread between ready-to-power campuses and speculative land banks may become one of the clearer signals in infrastructure credit.

## The Bottom Line

AI demand is reshaping finance, but the best underwriting still starts with physical bottlenecks.
