Manufacturers are building duplicate compliance teams as export controls become a standing operating cost.

## Why It Matters

The fastest teams are treating regulation as a logistics function, not a late-stage legal review.

That shift favors suppliers with cleaner documentation and shorter handoffs between design, testing, and shipping.

Watch for compliance tooling to become a selling point in chip manufacturing proposals.
